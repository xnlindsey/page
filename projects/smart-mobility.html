Can we trust
the city?

An emerging research direction connecting transit signal priority, microtransit, pedestrians, micromobility, and the reliability of infrastructure data.

Question

Connected infrastructure needs trusted information.

Modern transportation systems increasingly depend on data from vehicles, signals, sensors, maps, and people. This project explores how those data streams can be coordinated while accounting for uncertainty, cybersecurity, resilience, and the real behavior of people moving through a city.
