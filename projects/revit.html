Performance

Making carbon visible.

Autodesk Insight was used to explore operational and embodied carbon. The study estimated approximately 300 kWh/m²/yr operational energy, 6.7M kgCO₂e operational emissions, and 400k kgCO₂e embodied emissions over the modeled scenario.
