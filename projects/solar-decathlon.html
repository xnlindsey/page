Big Island
Basin.

A sustainable 1,800 ft² residential concept for Hawaiʻi, developed around climate, local materials, energy, water, and place.

Overview

Designing for the place, not just the plan.

The concept explored cross ventilation, low-e glazing, on-site energy generation, rainwater filtration, and material choices suited to the local environment. The work also considered Hawaiian architectural character and local customs.
